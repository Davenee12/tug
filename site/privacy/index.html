Privacy

tug collects nothing.

tug has no servers and no accounts. It talks to your iPhone over Bluetooth and keeps what it receives on your PC. Nothing is sent to the people who make tug.

This page is the short, human version. The full policy, which is the one that counts, is docs/PRIVACY.md, and its history is public.

What tug doesn't do

  • No tug servers, no sign-up, no account.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • Nothing you receive (notifications, texts, contacts, calls, files) is sent anywhere by tug.
  • No update checks to anyone but GitHub, where tug's releases live.

What stays on your PC

Your notifications, texts, contacts, recent calls and settings live in a database in your Windows profile (%APPDATA%\dev.davejames.tug), so you can see and search your history. Contact photos, app icons and album art are cached there too. Files you receive with Tugboat go to your Pictures › Tugboat folder.

tug's logs record connection events and errors, never message text or contact names. Copy diagnostics copies recent logs to your clipboard only when you press it, with phone numbers, email addresses, names, Bluetooth addresses and your phone's name removed.

Features that stay local

  • Tugboat runs on your local network only while its panel is open. Everything is encrypted with a key from the QR code, only the phone that scanned it can join, and it turns itself off after 10 idle minutes. Your clipboard is read only when you press Send clipboard, and never saved.
  • Voice typing is off by default. It listens only while you hold its shortcut, recognizes speech on your PC, and never sends or saves the recording.
  • Bring in your full history reads an iPhone backup that's already on your PC, and goes nowhere online. An encrypted backup's password is used once, in memory, and never saved.
  • Developer tools are off by default and reachable only by programs on your PC running as you. AI tools see only what you switch on, and a text or file is sent only after you click Send in tug.
  • Hide previews while sharing or presenting looks only at this PC, to spot a screen share or a slide show. It never keeps or logs what it sees.

Deleting your data

  • In tug: Settings › Data & privacy › Clear history deletes saved notifications and imported history; Settings › Messages › Remove imported history removes what came from a backup; Start over (Settings › iPhone) unpairs the phone.
  • Everything: uninstall tug, then delete %APPDATA%\dev.davejames.tug and %LOCALAPPDATA%\dev.davejames.tug if they're left. The full policy lists every location.
  • To remove the PC from your iPhone: Settings › Bluetooth, tap ⓘ next to this PC, then Forget This Device.

Security

tug treats everything it receives from the phone as untrusted, keeps Tugboat encrypted and local, and keeps the developer bridge to your Windows account. What it defends against, and what it doesn't try to, is in docs/SECURITY-MODEL.md. To report a vulnerability privately, see SECURITY.md.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Its fonts are served from the site itself, and it loads nothing from anyone else. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub handles the requests your browser makes to it, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
